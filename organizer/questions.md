# Problem 2 — The three questions

The program director wants answers to these. Each answer must come with a stated level of confidence and the reasoning behind that confidence.

**At least one of these three cannot be answered from this data.** Part of your task is working out which, and saying precisely what would be needed to answer it.

---

### Question 1

**Have case closure times increased between 2023 and 2025, and if so, by how much?**

The director's impression is that cases are taking noticeably longer to close than they used to. She would like to know whether that impression is correct, and if it is, the size of the change.

### Question 2

**If closure times have changed, what is driving the change?**

She is not looking for a theory. She is looking for whatever the data can actually attribute — a district, a case type, a period, a combination. If the change is concentrated somewhere specific, she needs to know where, because that determines what she does about it.

### Question 3

**Did the case triage process introduced during 2024 reduce closure times for high-priority cases?**

A new triage process was rolled out during 2024. The intention was that high-priority cases would be identified sooner and closed faster. She would like to know whether it worked.

---

## What we need from you

For each question:

- The answer, or a clear statement that the data cannot support one.
- Your confidence, and why you hold it.
- The assumptions the answer rests on.

And separately, the cleaning log required by the floor: every row you dropped or transformed, and why.
